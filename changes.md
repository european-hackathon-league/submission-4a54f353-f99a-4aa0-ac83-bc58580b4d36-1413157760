## Phase 5: scoring

| # | Change | Reason | Decided by |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5.1 | Factor normalizers: distinct customers = log(1 + n) / log(1 + max n of the config's cases); severity = (defects x 1.0 + difficult to use x 0.7 + other complaints x 0.7 + wishes x 0.4) / opinions; satisfaction gap = max over the feature's study attributes of (1 - normalized) / 0.3, or US dissatisfied share / 0.2, capped at 1; competitor gap = 1.0 (rivals offer it, not in our list), 0.6 (optional here), 0.4 (standard on some variants), 0.2 (standard), 0 without competitor evidence; market volume = 2030 units of the config market / largest market of the line; removed vs predecessor = 0 or 1; reach = log of capped reach / log(cap); trend = trend signals / 5, capped at 1. | Every factor in 0..1, explainable in one sentence; the rationale prints raw value, normalized value, weight and contribution per factor. | Claude |
